CAPITAL FARM
NPC Negotiation Mechanics
Deterministic Deal Progress, Price Movement, and Close Deal Gauge
Implementation specification for EconomyGame / Capital Farm Arcade ModeDefines how ask price, species, business situation, reputation, relationship memory, morality, leverage, conversation tactics, and offer terms produce a transparent and testable negotiation result.This document supersedes the earlier open-ended NPC utility model for the streamlined negotiation flow described here.
Version 2.0  |  July 2026  |  EconomyGame 0.6 -> 1.0
1. Executive Summary
The revised negotiation system converts free-form dialogue into a bounded, deterministic progression model. The AI may interpret what the player said and write the NPC response, but it may not decide whether the deal succeeds, how far the price moves, or what persistent state changes occur.
Core rule  Every negotiation begins with an asking price, a hard economic floor, a hidden species discount capacity, a hidden business-situation discount capacity, and a visible Deal Momentum gauge. Conversation unlocks movement inside those pre-generated ranges; it never creates a new range.
The system has two independent gates:
Economic gate: the player’s risk-adjusted offer value must meet or exceed the NPC’s current acceptable economic value.
Willingness gate: the visible Deal Momentum gauge must reach the Ready to Close zone without violating a red line.
Both gates must be satisfied before the Close Deal button becomes available.

1.2 Design Objectives
Make every result reproducible from stored values and classified player actions.
Give species a clear tactical identity without allowing species to override economics.
Show direction of progress without revealing hidden price floors or exact modifier values.
Make good discovery, structured offers, reputation, relationships, and leverage all matter.
Keep the player—not the AI—in control of final execution through a Close Deal action.
2. Authoritative Negotiation Model
The negotiation engine must maintain two related but separate state values throughout the encounter.
2.1 Economic State
Ask Price: the public starting price or reference value.
Hard Economic Floor: the minimum acceptable risk-adjusted value. It is generated before dialogue and never shown normally.
Species Discount Capacity: a hidden maximum selected within the species range.
Situation Discount Capacity: a hidden maximum selected from the business situation.
Unlocked Species Progress: 0-100%, moved by species-compatible conversation and terms.
Unlocked Situation Progress: 0-100%, moved by situation-compatible conversation and terms.
Leverage Economic Adjustment: a small deterministic adjustment to the total discount capacity, bounded and floor-limited.
Current Acceptable Economic Value: the value the current offer must meet.
2.2 Willingness State
Deal Momentum Gauge: visible 0-100 measure of how close the NPC is to agreeing.
Reputation Starting Modifier: derived from the existing reputation tier.
Relationship Memory Modifier: -25 to +25 gauge points.
Morality Compatibility Modifier: -30 to +30 gauge points when enabled.
Leverage Gauge Modifier: -20 to +20 gauge points.
Conversation Movement: positive or negative movement caused by validated tactics.
Offer Quality Movement: movement caused by how the current offer compares with the acceptable value.
Patience and Red-Line State: tracks repetition, severe mistakes, and immediate-failure conditions.
Separation rule  A warm conversation can improve willingness but cannot make an inadequate offer acceptable. A rich offer can satisfy the economic gate but still fail if the player has damaged trust, violated a red line, or exhausted the NPC’s patience.

3.3 Hard Floor and Global Discount Cap
The hard floor must be derived from economics, not personality. The combined species, situation, and leverage movement can never reduce acceptable value below the hard floor.
Current acceptable economic valueP_current = max(Hard Floor, Ask Price x (1 - Unlocked Discount))For seller acquisitions. Other roles use an equivalent minimum contract value, maximum cost, or risk-adjusted threshold.
Default global headline-discount cap: 35% of Ask Price unless a hand-authored distressed scenario explicitly permits more. The hard floor remains authoritative even when the theoretical modifiers exceed the cap.

7. Economic Calculation
7.1 Total Unlocked Discount
Unlocked discount before capsD_raw = Species Capacity x Species Progress + Situation Capacity x Situation Progress + Leverage Economic AdjustmentFor Stable Position, multiply the species portion by 0.75 before adding leverage.
Final unlocked discountD_final = min(Global Discount Cap, D_raw, 1 - Hard Floor / Ask Price)This ensures the acceptable value can never pass the hard floor.
7.2 Current Acceptable Value
Seller / acquisitionAcceptable Value = max(Hard Floor, Ask Price x (1 - D_final))
For other roles, translate the same logic into the relevant economic direction:

7.4 Species Preference Bonus
Species preference affects how the NPC values valid terms, but it cannot invent value. Cap the total species preference bonus at 8% of Ask Price or the equivalent contract reference value.
No free persuasion value  Social proof, warmth, confidence, and evidence primarily unlock discount progress and gauge movement. They are not added as imaginary dollars unless they are attached to an actual commercial term or risk reduction.

8.4 Display Rules
Show a five-zone horizontal gauge with the current marker and a small arrow indicating movement from the previous message.
Do not show exact gauge numbers by default; accessibility settings may optionally display a qualitative label.

9. Conversation Processing
Each player message must pass through the following authoritative sequence. This is the core implementation process.
Classify intent: discovery question, social/rapport, offer, counteroffer, acceptance, rejection, walk-away, threat, or irrelevant text.
Extract only explicitly stated terms: price, cash at closing, deferred amount, rate, duration, earn-out, volume, exclusivity, guarantees, employment commitments, and other supported fields.
Classify approved tactic tags. The AI may suggest tags, but deterministic validation confirms that the text supports them.
Apply repetition, contradiction, truth, and credibility checks against prior messages and known game facts.
Update Species Progress and Situation Progress using the approved movement rules.
Update temporary Conversation Movement on the Deal Momentum gauge.
If an offer exists, calculate Risk-Adjusted Offer Value.
Recalculate Total Unlocked Discount and Current Acceptable Economic Value.
Recalculate Offer Quality Movement and the visible Deal Momentum gauge.
Check hard red lines, patience, lowball history, and maximum exchanges.
Select the engine result: Continue, Counter, Ready to Close, Reject, or Walk-Away confirmation.
Provide the result and permitted facts to the AI, which writes one to three concise in-character sentences.
Update the visible structured offer summary and gauge trend.

9.2 Discovery
Discovery questions may reveal motivation, alternatives, risk, nonfinancial interests, decision process, or economics. A discovery answer can unlock a tactic route, modify the permitted offer terms, or reduce uncertainty. It does not directly change the hard floor unless the revealed fact was already encoded as a contingent economic rule.

10. Outcome Rules
10.1 Ready to Close
Ready conditionOffer Value >= Current Acceptable Value  AND  Gauge >= 75  AND  No Red Line Violated
When the condition is met, the NPC may say they are willing. The game displays Close Deal. No money, ownership, contract, or memory state changes until the player presses the button.
10.2 Counteroffer
Return Counter when the negotiation is still viable but one or both gates are not met. The counteroffer must be generated from the current acceptable value, preferred terms, and concession style; it may not be invented by the AI.
Economic gap small: counter near the current acceptable value.
Willingness low but economics sufficient: request trust-building, protection, or preferred terms rather than merely raising price.
Species or situation route partly unlocked: counter should expose the next meaningful path without revealing hidden percentages.
10.3 Rejection
Trigger
Result
Hard red line violated
Immediate rejection unless the term can be withdrawn before execution and the scenario explicitly permits repair.
Gauge reaches 0-14
Reject after the current response.
Two severe lowballs
Reject unless the NPC is a Goat or Cash Pressure situation with specific authored tolerance.
Credibility failure
Repeated contradiction or false claim may trigger rejection regardless of price.
Patience exhausted
Reject when final terms remain materially below threshold or willingness remains below 55.
10.4 Walk Away
The player may walk away at any time. Walking away is not automatically a failure. It may preserve reputation when the player avoids an uneconomic deal, but it may damage relationship memory when abandoning an urgent supplier/client issue or a promise already made.

12.2 Visibility Rules
Never reveal the exact hard floor, species capacity, situation capacity, progress percentages, memory number, morality number, or leverage score in normal play.

Negotiation Analyst or equivalent traits may reveal tighter estimates but still not the exact hidden state unless explicitly designed as a powerful late-game ability.
The structured offer summary must be shown before execution so the player can detect extraction errors.

The AI cannot move the gauge, change discount capacity, alter the hard floor, accept a deal, or create persistent terms.
Economic integrity
No negotiation closes below the hard floor after risk-adjusted term valuation.
Species clarity
Each species has at least three positive and three negative tactics that produce measurably different progress.
Situation clarity
The same species behaves differently in Cash Pressure, Retirement, Entrepreneur, and Stable situations.
Gauge honesty
Gauge direction corresponds to engine movement and never shows Ready when the economic gate fails.
Memory integrity
Relationship memory affects starting gauge only and updates only from validated outcomes.
Morality readiness
Morality modifier is stored as zero and can later be activated without changing the negotiation formulas.
Leverage integrity
Leverage derives from game state; player dialogue cannot fabricate it.
Offer extraction
The player reviews a structured summary before any state-changing close.
Walk-away viability
At least some scenarios are intentionally better to decline than to close.
Balance testing
All six species are tested in all four situations, across at least three reputation tiers and three leverage levels.
15. Final Implementation Process
The complete deal-metric process is:
1. Generate and store Ask Price, Hard Floor, species capacity, situation capacity, reputation, memory, morality, leverage, red lines, and permitted terms.
2. Initialize Species Progress, Situation Progress, patience, and Deal Momentum gauge.
3. Process each player message into validated intent, tactics, and explicit terms.
4. Update species and situation progress using authored weights and repetition rules.
5. Value the current offer, including deferred and non-cash terms.
6. Recalculate unlocked discount and current acceptable economic value.
7. Recalculate Deal Momentum from persistent modifiers, conversation movement, offer quality, discovery, and penalties.
8. Apply red lines and patience checks.
9. Select Continue, Counter, Ready to Close, Reject, or Walk Away.
10. Allow the AI to express that engine-selected result in character.
11. Execute only when the player presses Close Deal, then apply money, ownership, contract, memory, promise, reputation, and future-cost effects.
Definition of the revised system  Ask Price defines the starting anchor. Species and Situation define bounded discount lanes. Reputation, Memory, Morality, and Leverage define starting willingness. Conversation unlocks those lanes and moves the gauge. Offer economics determine whether the terms are acceptable. The rules engine decides the result; the AI performs the character.
