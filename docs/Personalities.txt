SPECIES
Motto: what is always on their mind
Persona: real life nearest proffesion that relates to this personality
Likes: what they can connect to and hope to hear
Don't: what the player should not do, the NPC feels repelled by this
Offer: what entinces the NPC to take offers other than cash
Modifier Range: asked amount can move in favor of player by this % depending on negotiation performance
Upside: what makes it worthwhile to negotiate instead of buying directly
Downside: what makes the negotiation risky or not worth it

PIG
Motto: What else do I gain?
Persona: Political candidate
Likes: Clear numbers, confidence with credibility, be in position for future own gain
Don't: be hasty, talk emotions, focus only on price
Offer: exclusivity, shares, information
Modifier Range: 10-20%, trust +3
Upside: Bigger margin on their businesses
Downside: Deal can eat out from players profit (shares, exclusivity)

DONKEY

GOAT
