## Locked integration decisions

### 1. Personal relationship → Deal Momentum gauge

**Decision:** Per-NPC **personal relationship** is a separate modifier from deal-history `relationshipMemory`. It behaves like reputation (species-weighted gauge contribution) but is scoped to one NPC.

| Concept | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Storage | One summarized score per player↔NPC, recommended range **-5 … +5** (data-driven) |
| Neutral default | **+0** — player can reach gauge 100 without any prior social prep |
| Gauge effect | **Additional** starting gauge points, not a gate |
| Species weight | Each species maps relationship points → gauge points (personality-driven) |

**Formula (negotiation bridge):**

```
personalGaugeAdj = round(personalRelationshipScore × speciesPersonalGaugeWeight[speciesId])
gaugeStart = clamp(
  GAUGE_BASE
  + reputationGaugeAdj
  + memoryGaugeAdj
  + leverageGaugeAdj
  + moralityGaugeAdj          // 0 until morality ships
  + personalGaugeAdj,          // NEW — community social prep
  0, 100
)
```

**Example weights (balancing defaults, not final rules):**

| Species | `speciesPersonalGaugeWeight` | At max +5 relationship |
|---------|------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Sheep | 4 | +20 gauge |
| Donkey | 2 | +10 gauge |
| Pig | 2 | +10 gauge |
| Horse | 3 | +15 gauge |
| Goat | 2 | +10 gauge |
| Hen | 1 | +5 gauge |

Sheep are more socially influenced; Donkeys less so — exact numbers live in `community_config.json`.

**UI:** Show qualitative relationship label (e.g. *Stranger*, *Friendly*, *Trusted*) and, after negotiation, a tooltip listing contributing factors including personal relationship. Do **not** expose raw scores in normal play.

**Source dimensions:** Chat still updates the full social state (familiarity, warmth, trust, etc.) from the Community spec. A deterministic summarizer rolls those into `personalRelationshipScore` before negotiation. Deal-history memory (`promisesKept`, grievances, last deal quality) remains separate and continues to use `summarize_memory_gauge()`.

### 2. Business selection — chain skeletons + interconnectivity

**Decision:** Do **not** replace authored district parcel layouts. Use existing **chain skeletons** from `farm_content.json` / supply catalog to **select ~20 businesses at random** per district generation run, with a **connectivity constraint** (≥15 businesses with at least one valid supply edge in the district graph).

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|

| Compatibility rules | `CONNECTIONS` / template input-output tags — engine-validated, never LLM-decided |
| District slots | Meadowgate parcel grid assigns generated businesses to parcels |
| Determinism | World seed + generator version → same community on reload |

Vacant / development lots in Meadowgate receive generated businesses. Bank, civic, and fixed special roles may remain authored anchors.

### 3. Notebook — single UI, two intel channels

**Decision:** All discovered intel lands in the **negotiation notebook** (same panel as seller diligence today). Source and category distinguish content.

| Source | Cost | Notebook content | Negotiation use |
|--------|------|------------------|-----------------|
| **Investigate** (1 AP) | 1 AP | Negotiation tactics: expected phrases, hard-floor hints, *moved most by*, species/situation alignment, unlock phrases | Direct tactic routing in active negotiation |
| **Chat** (social visit) | Free (see §4) | Hidden leverage: supplier/client opportunities, likes/fears, operational facts, preferences, relationship context | Feeds `leverageScore`, personal relationship, and contextual dialogue — not the Investigate phrase list |

**Rules:**

- Investigate and chat intel **stack**; neither replaces the other.
- Chat can surface leverage that improves negotiation posture; Investigate remains the path for **structured negotiation tactics**.
- Player may enter negotiation without Investigate, but phrase unlocks and floor hints are weaker unless learned socially or through play.

**Notebook record shape (minimum):**

```json
{
  "fact_id": "stable_id",
  "display_summary": "Player-readable text",
  "source": "investigate | chat",
  "category": "tactic | floor_hint | leverage | preference | operational | supplier_client",
  "source_npc_id": "npc_id or null",
  "confidence": "rumored | supported | confirmed",
  "discovery_turn": 4,
  "negotiation_tags": ["supplier_leverage", "warm_rapport"]
}
```

### 7. Ownership transfer — supply clients + personal relationship

**Decision:** When the player acquires a business, **commercial supply relationships transfer with the business**. **Personal NPC relationships do not transfer** but shape how counterparts treat the new owner.

#### What transfers (business record)

- Active supplier/client contracts from district supply graph
- Reliability, dependence, payment terms, open disputes
- Operational facts tied to the edge (late deliveries, quality issues, spare capacity)

#### What does not transfer (NPC record)

- Player↔prior-owner social state stays on the NPC entity
- Counterpart NPCs retain their view of the **player**, not the previous owner

#### Post-acquisition client behavior

Major clients defined by the district supply chain may **trigger a renegotiation event** shortly after acquisition. Terms depend on **both** inherited contract state **and** player↔client personal relationship:

| Inherited contract health | Personal relationship | Example behavior |
|---------------------------|----------------------|------------------|
| Strong / reliable | Good (+3 to +5) | Client asks for **~30% additional capacity over 3 quarters**, friendly terms, review at end of period |
| Strong / reliable | Neutral | Standard renewal; modest volume ask |
| Strained / unreliable | Bad (-3 to -5) | Client demands **~50% of your capacity** or **termination** if not met; strict payment/delivery covenants |
| Strained | Good | Willing to renegotiate; softer thresholds but still addresses past failures |

These scenarios are **authored templates** parameterized by contract metrics + `personalRelationshipScore`. Engine validates fulfillment each turn; broken commitments affect trust, resentment, and future gauge.

### 8. Chat-discovered leverage → negotiation

**Decision:** Covered by §1 and §3.

- Chat facts tagged `leverage`, `supplier_client`, or `operational` feed **`leverageScore`** (0–1) and qualitative leverage label (Weak → Dominant).
- Strong/Dominant leverage still subject to Negotiation v2 rules: gauge adj (-20..+20), small economic adj, reputation / relationship penalty if used aggressively.
- Personal relationship affects **gauge** via species weight; discovered facts affect **leverage** channel — both visible in post-negotiation modifier snapshot.

### 9. Persistence

**Decision:** **No SQLite hard requirement.** Use structured game persistence (RunState / save JSON) with the same logical schema as the Community spec appendix. Requirements:

- Fast lookup by `npc_id`, `business_id`, `fact_id`, `district_id`
- Append-only interaction events where practical
- Schema version + generator version on save for migration

SQLite remains an acceptable implementation choice if query complexity grows; not mandatory for MVP.

## Promise type catalog (initial)

Promises are dialogue-initiated but **engine-verified** against game state. Status: `proposed → accepted → fulfilled | broken | expired | canceled`.

| `promise_type` | Fulfillment check (engine) | Typical consequence if broken |
|----------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| `retain_employees` | Headcount / labor track unchanged ≥ N turns after acquisition | Resentment +; Horse penalty amplified |
| `pay_invoice_on_time` | Cash payment to NPC/vendor by deadline turn | Trust --; supplier leverage shifts |
| `use_local_supplier` | Supply policy set to named local supplier for M turns | Business respect -- |
| `return_with_information` | Player discovers and notebook-records specified fact by deadline | Trust --; disclosure eligibility drops |
| `capacity_commitment_soft` | Allocate ≥ X% spare capacity to client for `duration_turns` | Relationship repair opportunity if met |
| `capacity_commitment_strict` | Allocate ≥ X% capacity or contract enters breach state | Termination event; reputation hit |
| `delivery_reliability` | Supplier link strain below threshold for N turns | Gratitude +; leverage improves |
| `price_hold` | Offer price unchanged for client/supplier for N turns | Trust + if met |
| `exclusive_supply` | No alternate supplier policy for tagged input for N turns | Strong gratitude or strong resentment |
| `renegotiate_after_period` | Scheduled renegotiation event fires; player engages | Neutral if honored; resentment if ignored |

Promise proposals from the model must use IDs from this catalog (or `community_config.json` extension). Unknown types are rejected at validation.

Aligns `NegotiationV2Profile.build()` with community social prep:

```
gaugeStart = clamp(
  GAUGE_BASE                           // 50
  + reputationTier.gaugeAdj              // -5 .. +10
  + summarize_memory_gauge(counterparty) // -25 .. +25  (deal history)
  + leverage_from_score(score).gaugeAdj// -20 .. +20
  + moralityGaugeAdj                     // 0 (future)
  + personalRelationshipGaugeAdj(npc),   // NEW: score × speciesWeight
  0, 100
)
```

## Acceptance criteria (Meadowgate MVP)

1. **Generation:** Fixed seed recreates same 20-business graph, NPCs, and facts.
2. **Chat:** Map click on non-sale business opens chat; 5 messages then dismiss; Ollama required.
3. **Notebook:** Investigate and chat intel appear in one notebook with correct categories.
4. **Negotiation:** Personal relationship and chat leverage appear in gauge snapshot; neutral relationship contributes +0. *(Phase 4)*
5. **Acquisition:** Buying a business inherits supply edges; major client may spawn renegotiation event per relationship + contract health templates. *(Phase 5)*
6. **Integrity:** Model cannot mutate money, ownership, or scores directly; all effects traceable in debug log.
